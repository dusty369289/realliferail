About

Real Life Rail shows where your train is between stations. Realtime Trains records the time each train passes every timing point: stations, junctions and the stations it runs through. Between those reports, this app places the train on the line at the booked speed for that section.

Limits, plainly

  • Signals and track circuits aren't in Realtime Trains' data, only timing points. Between two timing points, where the train is shown is our estimate.
  • Reports are to the minute, so a train can be up to a minute ahead of or behind where the bar shows it.
  • Times marked est are ours. Everything else comes from Realtime Trains.
